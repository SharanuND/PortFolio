About Me

A little about my professional journey

I am an Embedded Firmware Engineer with experience in developing firmware for microcontroller-based products and connected devices.

My technical experience includes Embedded C/C++, bare-metal programming, RTOS, device drivers, communication protocols, Bluetooth technologies, firmware debugging and IoT integration.

I have worked with platforms such as STM32, ESP32, NXP S32K3XX and Cypress/Infineon Bluetooth devices. I enjoy solving hardware-software integration challenges and developing dependable firmware solutions.
